# Review Queue — spaced repetition

> **The least glamorous and least skippable file here.** Retention is biological; no amount of AI changes it.
>
> Every problem you solve goes in. Re-solve from a **blank file**, timed, at D+1 / D+3 / D+7 / D+21.
>
> **A failed re-solve resets to D+1.** No negotiation, no "I almost had it".
>
> **Hard stop:** two review-queue skips in a row and new material pauses until the queue is clear. This is the only hard stop in the plan (`PLAN.md` §9).
