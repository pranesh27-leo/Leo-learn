# Divide and Conquer

Hello Algo **Ch 12**: [Divide and conquer](https://www.hello-algo.com/en/chapter_divide_and_conquer/divide_and_conquer/).

Split problem into subproblems, solve recursively, combine.

**Examples:** merge sort, quick sort, binary search, closest pair (advanced).

**Complexity:** often O(n log n) when split in half and combine is O(n).
